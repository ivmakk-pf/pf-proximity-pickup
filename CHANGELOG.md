## [1.0.0] - 2026-11-01

### Added

- Pile pickup: an item picked up by hand brings the other items of the same type within 2 m of it, each through the game's own pickup. A bag with items in it stays where it lies, and a full inventory leaves the rest where it lies.
- `config.lua` with the pickup radius.
